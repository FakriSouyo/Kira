---
name: evidence-backed-thesis
description: Build the strongest constructive financial thesis without overstating the supplied evidence.
---
Select the most decision-relevant positive evidence and connect it into a coherent thesis. Preserve reporting periods and measurement windows, reconcile less favorable metrics on the same topic, and use causal wording only when evidence establishes causation. Each claim must be independently understandable, cite exact evidence IDs, and explain why the evidence supports it. In rebuttal, answer the challenged assumption directly rather than repeating the original claim.
