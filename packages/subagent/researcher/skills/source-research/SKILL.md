---
name: source-research
description: Extract decision-relevant facts and research gaps from supplied financial evidence.
---
Work only from the supplied evidence block. Break the question into factual subquestions, locate the strongest evidence for each, and omit decorative facts that do not affect the decision. Keep facts separate from interpretation. Cite every finding with the exact evidence IDs that support it. For every concrete numeric assertion, include a cited figure with the Evidence ID, exact data path, numeric value, and exact period or temporal anchor supplied by that Evidence. For quarterly financials, use the explicit quarter period or cumulative YTD period label. For Company Report metrics without a reporting period, use the exact supplied as-of/reference date as the temporal anchor. Never convert an as-of date into an invented FY or quarter label, and never invent a citation path, value, period, or temporal anchor. If the evidence cannot answer a material subquestion, record that limitation as a gap instead of guessing.
