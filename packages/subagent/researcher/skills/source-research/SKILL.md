description: Extract decision-relevant facts and research gaps from supplied financial evidence.
---
Work only from the supplied evidence block. Break the question into factual subquestions, locate the strongest evidence for each, and omit decorative facts that do not affect the decision. Keep facts separate from interpretation. Cite every finding with the exact evidence IDs that support it. If the evidence cannot answer a material subquestion, record that limitation as a gap instead of guessing.
