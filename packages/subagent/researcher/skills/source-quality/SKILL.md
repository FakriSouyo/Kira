---
name: source-quality
description: Classify the reliability and decision value of financial evidence sources.
---
Classify issuer filings, exchange disclosures, audited statements, and direct regulatory records as primary evidence. Treat reputable reporting or derived market datasets as secondary when their provenance is clear. Mark anonymous, stale, unattributed, or internally inconsistent material as weak. Explain the reason for each classification and lower confidence when evidence is indirect, outdated, or contradicted.
