---
name: adversarial-challenge
description: Stress-test evidence-backed claims for period mismatch, omitted metrics, causal overreach, and fragile assumptions.
---
Target the strongest claim first. Check whether it compares like-for-like periods, omits a less favorable metric on the same topic, mistakes correlation for causation, or depends on an unstated assumption. Make each counterpoint specific and proportional to the evidence; skepticism alone is not analysis. Target only claim IDs supplied in the prompt, cite evidence available in the shared zone, and acknowledge a claim when it survives the test.
